01 — Overview

What kind of service is PartnerFit?

PartnerFit is a service that uses AI to search for and recommend overseas buyer and supplier candidates based on your company and product information, and automatically produces an analysis report and tailored email drafts.

Not tied to specific countries or products, it searches worldwide for sales and supply sources at the same time. It analyzes your company type, product information and trade terms together, and shortlists only the partners that fit your current way of doing business.

Reference (basis and source): As an example of a paid overseas buyer-search service from a public agency, KOTRA's Export24 lists potential-buyer discovery at KRW 150,000 per company (excluding VAT). PartnerFit, by contrast, charges no service fee (AI usage costs are billed to your own API account). Source: KOTRA Export24 Global Agency (Overseas Market Research) Service Guide (based on terms effective Nov 2024)

Buyer discovery

Companies looking to expand overseas sales channels

When manufacturers, brand companies and trading companies look for overseas importers, distributors and industrial end-users.

Supplier discovery

Companies looking to secure overseas supply sources

When looking overseas for manufacturers and OEM/ODM suppliers of raw materials, components and finished products.

A shared challenge: gathering company information scattered across languages, comparing and shortlisting candidates, and preparing the first contact takes time and people. Even for the same product, the right counterpart differs depending on the company type and business model.

04 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is PartnerFit free?

Yes. PartnerFit itself is free to use. However, the API usage cost of running the AI is billed to your own AI provider account (OpenAI or Anthropic).

What is PartnerFit?

PartnerFit is a service that uses AI to search for and recommend overseas buyer and supplier candidates based on your company and product information, and automatically produces an analysis report and tailored email drafts.

How does buyer discovery differ from supplier discovery?

Buyer discovery finds importers, distributors, brand companies and industrial end-users that match your product and supply terms; supplier discovery finds manufacturers, raw-material and component suppliers, and OEM/ODM companies that match your purchasing terms. The evaluation criteria also differ: for buyers, the focus is the products they handle and their distribution or procurement methods; for suppliers, it is supply capability, specifications, volume and lead time.

What information do I need to enter?

You enter your company type, business model, manufacturing and supply capability, the products you want to sell or buy, specifications, price range, volume and certifications, and your target market and trade terms.

What do I receive as output?

You receive a report covering candidate company overviews, products handled, business roles, trade fit, reasons for recommendation, contact information and priorities, plus multilingual email drafts for sales proposals, purchase inquiries and quotation requests.

Can it only be used for specific countries or products?

No. It supports searching for sales and supply sources without limiting you to specific countries or products.
