Who it is built for

PartnerFit is an AI service that finds overseas buyer and supplier candidates. It picks only the candidates that match your criteria and shows why they match, with sources.

Buyer discovery

When you want to sell

When a manufacturer, brand or trading company looks for overseas importers, distributors, or companies that use the product.

Supplier discovery

When you want to source

When you look for overseas manufacturers and OEM/ODM partners to make raw materials, components or finished goods.

How it works

1

Enter your criteria

Describe the partner type and countries you want, plus your products and trade terms. Only a few fields are required.

2

AI searches the web

It searches the public web to gather candidates and filters out those it cannot support with evidence.

3

Scores and evidence

Candidates are scored on seven criteria, such as product fit, business role and credit clues, and each gets source links.

4

Report and email drafts

Save the report as a PDF. Each candidate's first-contact email draft is written in the language of the counterpart's country (Korean, English, Chinese or Japanese), and in English for all other countries.

What sets it apart

No candidate without evidence

Every candidate carries source links. Contact details that could not be verified are marked 'needs verification', and candidates without evidence are left out of the report.

Buyers and suppliers are judged differently

Buyers are weighed more on the products they handle and how they distribute; suppliers on supply capability, specifications, volume and lead time.

Runs on your own API key

The service itself is free. AI usage fees are separate from any ChatGPT or Claude monthly subscription: they are billed per use to your own OpenAI or Anthropic API account. Your API key is never stored.

Good to know

The AI judges only from public web information, so errors are possible. Credit standing is estimated from public clues and is not a credit check. Check the source links and company details yourself before making contact.

Frequently asked questions

Is PartnerFit free?

Yes. PartnerFit itself is free to use. However, running the AI has a separate API cost. It is independent of any ChatGPT or Claude monthly subscription and is billed per use to your own AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) API account.

What results do I get?

You get a report covering up to 10 candidates: overview, score, reasons for recommendation (with source links), publicly listed contact details, and an email draft for each candidate. You can save it as a PDF.

How does buyer discovery differ from supplier discovery?

Buyer discovery finds importers, distributors and end-user companies that would buy your product; supplier discovery finds manufacturers, component makers and OEM/ODM partners that can make what you need. The evaluation criteria differ too: buyers are judged mainly on the products they handle and their distribution method, suppliers on supply capability, specifications, volume and lead time.

What do I enter?

You enter the partner criteria you are looking for and your company and product details. Some fields are required and the rest are optional; the more detail you give, the more accurate the results.

Can I rely on the results as they are?

No. It is reference material that AI compiles from public web information. Check the source links and verify the company's details yourself before making contact. Items that could not be verified are marked 'needs verification'.

How is the information I enter used?

You use the service after signing in, and if you consent, your inputs and results are stored and analysed to improve matching quality. API keys are never stored. You can delete your records yourself with 'Delete my records' in the service screen.
